Tool Disclaimers

Last updated: 2026-09-03

The Short Version

These calculators are educational instruments. They produce estimates. They are not a substitute for a qualified professional who knows your specific situation, and no output from these tools creates a professional-client relationship. Verify anything material with someone whose job it is to be right about your case.

Financial Calculators

The finance calculators (mortgage, loan, compound interest, debt payoff, savings, ROI, FIRE, freelancer tax, side hustle, DCA, and any others in the same category) produce mathematical results based on the numbers you enter. They do not know your credit score, your local tax code, your existing debts, your income volatility, your risk tolerance, or the specific terms a lender or advisor would offer you. Outputs are estimates for planning purposes only — not financial, tax, investment, or legal advice. Verify with a licensed financial planner, tax professional, or advisor before making a decision that turns on the number.

Tax & Withholding Calculators

Any tool that displays tax rates or brackets uses published rate data for a specific tax year, labeled in the interface. Actual withholding depends on your W‑4 elections, state and local taxes, pre-tax deductions, and prior-year information the tool does not have. Estimates are not a substitute for a prepared return.

Insurance Calculators

Insurance estimates (life, term vs. whole, disability, auto) are for education only. They are not quotes, and they do not account for underwriting decisions or premium factors specific to you. Consult a licensed agent for coverage decisions or a real quote.

Health & Wellness Calculators

Health calculators (BMI, TDEE, macros, BMR, body fat, ideal weight, water intake, and similar) produce general-information estimates. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Individual health depends on factors these calculators cannot see. Consult a healthcare provider before acting on any output.

Construction & Materials Estimators

Material-quantity estimators (concrete, paint, flooring, lumber, fence posts, wallpaper, drywall, tile, gravel, mulch, shingles, and similar) compute typical quantities from the dimensions and options you provide. They do not account for site conditions, waste factors specific to your project, local building codes, or engineering requirements. Verify quantities before purchasing and consult a qualified contractor or engineer for structural or code questions. These tools do not perform structural or engineering analysis.

Data Vintage

Calculators that depend on dated inputs (tax brackets, exchange rates, LLM API prices) display the vintage of their reference data in the interface. Rates change; the tool may lag reality. Confirm current rates against a primary source before making a decision that depends on them.

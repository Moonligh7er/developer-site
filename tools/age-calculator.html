How age is calculated

Years and months are counted the way people count birthdays: you gain a year on each birthday and a month on the same day-number each month. When that day doesn't exist (a 31st in a 30-day month), the month's last day counts instead. The remaining days are counted from the most recent such date. Born on 1990-06-15 and measured on 2026-09-04, that's 36 years, 2 months and 20 days.

Totals are whole calendar days on the Gregorian calendar, so leap years are included and clock changes for daylight saving can't shift the count. The hours figure is simply days × 24 and ignores the time of birth.

Leap-day birthdays

If you were born on 29 February, there's no single rule for non-leap years. Many people celebrate on 28 February; some legal systems (England and Wales, for example) treat you as reaching a new age on 1 March. When the date of birth is 29 February, the tool shows a choice; 28 February is the default.

Time zones

“Today” is your device's local date. Dates are treated as calendar days, so being born in a different time zone from where you are now doesn't change the result.

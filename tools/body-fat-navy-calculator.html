Body Fat Calculator (Navy Method)

The US Navy circumference method estimates body fat from a few tape measurements: neck, waist and height (plus hips for women). It's often within about 3–4 percentage points of lab methods such as DEXA — more informative than BMI for one person, less precise than a scan. Results update as you type.

How the Navy method works

Researchers at the Naval Health Research Center (Hodgdon and Beckett, 1984) fitted body-fat equations to circumference measurements and height, using underwater weighing as the reference. Versions of them have long been used for military body-composition screening. The equations come in two published forms, and this page uses the one that matches your units:

Inches, male: 86.010 × log₁₀(waist − neck) − 70.041 × log₁₀(height) + 36.76
Inches, female: 163.205 × log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) − 97.684 × log₁₀(height) − 78.387
Centimetres, male: 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log₁₀(waist − neck) + 0.15456 × log₁₀(height)) − 450
Centimetres, female: 495 ÷ (1.29579 − 0.35004 × log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100 × log₁₀(height)) − 450

The centimetre form predicts body density and converts it with the Siri equation (495 ÷ density − 450). The two forms usually agree within a few tenths of a percentage point. The method tracks typical fat distribution well; it is less reliable at the extremes (very lean athletes, higher body fat, or fat carried unusually), where DEXA or underwater weighing are better.

ACE reference ranges (adult, general population)

Ranges from the American Council on Exercise chart: essential fat 2–5% (male) and 10–13% (female); athletes 6–13% / 14–20%; fitness 14–17% / 21–24%; average 18–24% / 25–31%; above that, 25%+ / 32%+. They describe where people commonly fall, not what anyone should aim for, and they don't adjust for age. Very low body fat is not a health goal for most people.

Measurement tips

Measure in the morning before eating. Neck: just below the larynx, tape sloping slightly downward to the front. Waist for the male equation: horizontally at the navel. Waist for the female equation: at the narrowest point between ribs and hips. Hips: around the widest part of the buttocks. Stand relaxed and don't pull in your stomach.

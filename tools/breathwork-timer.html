Box Breathing
4s - 4s - 4s - 4s
Four equal four-second phases: in, hold, out, hold. A common way to steady nerves.
4-7-8 Technique
4s - 7s - 8s
In for 4, hold for 7, out for 8. A long, slow exhale, popularised by Dr Andrew Weil.
Wim Hof-style Rounds
30 breaths + hold + recovery
30 full, quick breaths, a hold after breathing out, then a 15-second recovery hold. Sitting or lying down only.
Nadi Shodhana
Alternate nostril, 4s phases
Alternate-nostril breathing with short holds. Switch sides with your thumb and ring finger.

Go gently. Breathe in a way that stays comfortable. If you feel dizzy, light-headed, tingly or anxious, stop and let your breathing return to normal; the holds are always optional.

Quick-breathing rounds with long holds can make some people faint. Do them sitting or lying down, and never in or near water, in the bath, while driving, or anywhere fainting could hurt you. If you are pregnant, or have epilepsy, a heart condition or high blood pressure, check with a clinician first. Use Breathe now to end any hold early.

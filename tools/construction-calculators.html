Construction Calculators

Estimate materials for concrete, paint, flooring, lumber, fencing and wallpaper. Each tab shows its formula and assumptions, works in imperial or metric, and asks for nothing but numbers.

Concrete Calculator

Volume of concrete for a slab or footing, or for round columns and post holes, with ready-mix and pre-mixed bag counts.

Patios and walks 4 in; driveways 4–6 in.
Post holes usually go below the frost line.
Spillage, uneven ground, form bulge. 5–10% is common.

Estimate

How it works

volume = length × width × thickness (or π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × depth × count for round forms), plus the waste allowance. 1 yd³ = 27 ft³ = 0.7646 m³. Pre-mixed bag yields are the published figures for standard concrete mix: 80 lb → 0.60 ft³, 60 lb → 0.45 ft³, 40 lb → 0.30 ft³. Ready-mix trucks deliver in quarter-yard steps, so that line rounds up. For post holes, the post itself takes up a little room; the estimate ignores that, which leaves a small margin.

Paint Coverage Calculator

How much paint a room needs from its size, openings, coats and the coverage printed on the can.

From the can; most interior paint says 250–400 sq ft per gallon.

Estimate

How it works

walls = 2 × (length + width) × height, minus about 21 sq ft (1.95 m²) per door and 15 sq ft (1.39 m²) per window, plus the ceiling if ticked. Multiply by coats and divide by the can's coverage. The default 350 sq ft per gallon (about 8.6 m² per liter) is a typical label figure for smooth, primed walls; rough or porous surfaces and big color changes use more.

Flooring / Tile Calculator

Tiles or planks and boxes for a rectangular room, including the grout joint and a waste factor.

1/8 in = 0.125. Use 0 for click-lock planks.
10% straight; 15% diagonal or herringbone.

Estimate

How it works

Each piece covers (length + joint) × (width + joint). pieces = ceil(room area ÷ that × (1 + waste)), boxes = ceil(pieces ÷ per box). This is the same formula as the Tile calculator, which also works out thinset and grout, so the two pages agree for the same inputs.

Lumber Estimator

Boards and board feet for decking, a board fence, a framed wall or shelving.

The stock length you'll buy: 8, 10, 12, 16 ft…
3/16 in (about 5 mm) is typical for decking.

Estimate

How it works

Deck: rows = ceil(width ÷ (board width + gap)), each row ceil(length ÷ board length) boards, the same formula as the Deck calculator. Fence: one board per board width + gap, cut to fence height (several pieces per board when they fit). Wall: studs at 16 in on center plus one at the end, and three plate runs (one bottom, two top). Shelving: boards side by side to make the shelf depth, cut to shelf length.

Board feet use nominal sizes, the way lumber is priced: thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12, so a 2 × 6 × 8 ft is 8 board feet. Actual sizes are smaller (a 2 × 6 is 1.5 × 5.5 in); the actual width is what's used to count boards.

Fence Post Spacing Calculator

Posts, rails and pickets for a fence line.

6–8 ft is typical for wood fences.
Each corner can add a short section, so one extra section is counted per corner.
Use 0 for a privacy fence.
For splits, culls and cuts.

Estimate

How it works

sections = ceil(length ÷ spacing) + corners and posts = sections + 1 for a fence with two ends. If the fence closes on itself (a full loop), subtract one post. Two of the posts are end posts and each corner gets a corner post; the rest are line posts. rails = sections × rails per section; pickets = ceil(length ÷ (picket width + gap)) plus the extra. Gates need their own posts and hardware.

Wallpaper Calculator

Rolls of wallpaper by the strip method, with pattern repeat and trimming allowance.

US double roll 20.5 in × 33 ft; European roll 53 cm × 10.05 m.
0 for no repeat. Printed on the label.
About 2 in top and bottom for trimming.

Estimate

How it works

strips = ceil(perimeter ÷ roll width). Each strip is the wall height plus the trim allowance, rounded up to a whole number of pattern repeats so the design lines up. strips per roll = floor(roll length ÷ strip length) and rolls = ceil(strips ÷ strips per roll). Doors and windows only change the wall area shown: the short pieces above and below them rarely save a whole strip, so the roll count is for the full perimeter. That leaves a little spare for repairs.

Estimate onlyMaterial estimates for planning and pricing, not professional advice. Check quantities with your supplier, and local codes and a professional for anything structural. Full disclaimers.

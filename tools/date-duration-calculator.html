Date Duration Calculator

Enter two dates to get the time between them in days, weeks, months and years, plus how many are weekdays. Weekdays here means Monday to Friday; public holidays aren't known to the tool. Results update as you type.

Processing happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
Weekday counts skip Saturdays and Sundays only. Public and observed holidays vary by country, state and employer, so subtract those yourself.

Duration

How it counts

Days: by default the end date isn't counted, the way “days between” and most deadlines work: 2026-01-01 to 2026-01-02 is 1 day. Tick “include the end date” to count both ends (2 days), which suits things like hotel nights versus days on site, or leave periods.

Weekdays: Monday to Friday within the same span. Saturdays and Sundays are excluded; holidays are not. In the US, federal holidays alone are 11 days a year, and state or employer holidays add more.

Months and years: calendar-based, not 30- or 365-day blocks. 2026-01-15 to 2026-03-15 is exactly 2 months. A month is counted when the same day-number comes round again; if it doesn't exist that month, the month's last day counts (2026-01-31 to 2026-02-28 is 1 month). Anything left over is shown as days.

Leap years, time zones and DST

Dates are counted as whole calendar days on the Gregorian calendar, including 29 February in leap years. Clock changes for daylight saving can't shift the count, because no times of day are involved. The hours line is days × 24; a day with a clock change actually has 23 or 25 hours.

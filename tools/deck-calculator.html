Deck Materials Calculator

Enter the deck size, the decking board and the joist layout. You get decking boards, joists and fasteners, plus a rough check of the joist span against the IRC deck table. Materials only; not a structural design.

Deck

The direction the decking runs (usually parallel to the house).
The joists run this way, from ledger to beam.

Decking

2×6 and 5/4×6 = 5.5 in (14 cm); most composite = 5.5 in.
3/16 in = 0.1875 in (about 5 mm) is typical. Follow the maker for composites.
Common lengths: 8, 10, 12, 16, 20 ft.

Framing & fasteners

Estimate

Board and joist math

rows = ceil(width × 12 ÷ (board width + gap)); each row needs ceil(length ÷ board length) boards; the waste factor is added and the total rounded up. Joists: ceil(length × 12 ÷ spacing) + 1, each as long as the deck is wide. Screws: two per board at every joist crossing; hidden clips: one. The Lumber tab on the hub page uses the same decking formula.

Joist span check (rough guidance)

The check compares your deck width, treated as a simple span from ledger to one beam with no cantilever, with IRC Table R507.6. Those spans assume No. 2 grade, wet service, 40 psf live load plus 10 psf dead load (and snow load no greater than 40 psf). Higher snow loads, other grades, cantilevers, hot tubs and planters all shorten the span. Beams (Table R507.5), posts, footings, ledger attachment and guards have their own tables. Use this as a first look, then check the current code your jurisdiction has adopted, the AWC Prescriptive Residential Wood Deck Construction Guide (DCA 6), or a deck builder or engineer.

Fasteners

Two screws per board per joist is the usual face-screw pattern. Hidden clip systems use one clip per crossing plus starter clips at the edges; check the maker's count per box.

Source: 2021 IRC Table R507.6, deck joist spans for common lumber species (no cantilever). Checked November 2026.

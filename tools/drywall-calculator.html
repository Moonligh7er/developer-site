Drywall Calculator

Measure a room or type in total areas. You get sheets for the size you pick, plus screws, joint compound and tape. The waste factor is yours to set.

Room

Each door takes off about 21 sq ft (1.95 m²).
Each window takes off about 15 sq ft (1.39 m²).

Areas

Sheets & waste

10% for a plain rectangular room; 15% when there are lots of cuts (closets, short walls, angled ceilings).

Estimate

How it works

Room mode: walls = 2 × (length + width) × height − 21 sq ft per door − 15 sq ft per window, plus length × width for the ceiling. Then sheets = ceil(area × (1 + waste) ÷ sheet area), always rounded up to whole sheets.

Finishing materials

  • Joint compound: about 10 gallons of ready-mixed all-purpose compound per 1,000 sq ft of board (USG), sold in 4.5-gallon pails.
  • Tape: about 370 ft of paper tape per 1,000 sq ft of board (USG), in 250 or 500 ft rolls.
  • Screws: roughly one screw per square foot (32 per 4 × 8 sheet). That follows from typical spacing: 16 in on wall studs and 12 in on ceiling joists, the IRC maximums for screws. A 1 lb box of 1¼ in screws holds roughly 250–300; check the box.

Finishing materials are based on the board area itself, not the waste allowance.

Bigger sheets save labor

4 × 12 sheets leave fewer joints to tape and mud, so pros usually pick the largest sheet that fits through the door and up the stairs. 4 × 8 is easier to handle alone.

Sources: USG Sheetrock paper joint tape and all-purpose compound coverage figures; IRC Table R702.3.5 fastener spacing. Checked November 2026.

Estimate onlyMaterial quantities for planning and pricing. Openings, ceiling height and layout change real waste. Full disclaimers.

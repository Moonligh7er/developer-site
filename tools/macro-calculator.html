Macro basics

Calories from each macro: protein and carbs = 4 kcal/g, fat = 9 kcal/g. So a 30/40/30 split on 2,000 kcal gives 150g protein (600 kcal), 200g carbs (800 kcal), 67g fat (600 kcal).

When each preset fits

Balanced 30/40/30: general-purpose maintenance or slow recomp, most people. Higher-protein 40/35/25: weight loss (preserves lean mass during a deficit) or hypertrophy training. Low-carb 35/20/45: keto-adjacent, ketogenic diets, or people who feel better on lower carbs. Endurance 25/55/20: athletes doing 8+ hours/week of cardio — glycogen replenishment matters. These are common conventions, not prescriptions; individual needs vary.

Protein per pound of bodyweight

A common lifter's target is 0.8–1.0g protein per lb of bodyweight. Higher-end research (Morton et al., meta-analysis) puts the ceiling around 1.6g/kg (~0.73g/lb) for muscle building — beyond that, extra protein doesn't add muscle gains, though it can help satiety during a cut. If your macro split gives you significantly more or less than your bodyweight-based target, use custom mode to dial in the exact grams.

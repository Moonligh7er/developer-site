Paste any message, pitch, post, or speech. The engine names the techniques it finds — coercion, scam patterns, gaslighting language, fallacies, propaganda framing, urgency, weasel wording — explains each one, and tells you what to do next.

Nothing you paste leaves this page. The engine is the same rule set that powers Filament: Persuasion Lab: it detects structure (if-you-don’t/I-will threats, pay-then-do bribes, ultimatums) as well as vocabulary, weighs each hit by how harmful the technique is, and reports its own confidence. Honest hedging (“perhaps”, “to the best of my knowledge”) is never counted against a writer.

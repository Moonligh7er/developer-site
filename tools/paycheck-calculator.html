    How this calculator works

    Your pay is annualized. Pre-tax 401(k) contributions, HSA/FSA and health premiums come off before federal income tax; HSA/FSA and premiums taken through a cafeteria (Section 125) plan also escape Social Security and Medicare, but 401(k) deferrals don't. The 2026 standard deduction for your filing status is subtracted, and what's left runs through the 2026 brackets. Social Security is 6.2% of wages up to $184,500; Medicare is 1.45% of all wages, plus 0.9% on wages above $200,000 (single, head of household), $250,000 (joint) or $125,000 (separate). Employers start withholding that extra 0.9% at $200,000 regardless of status, so the true-up for some couples happens on the tax return.

    2026 figures and sources

    Brackets and standard deductions ($16,100 single and separate, $32,200 joint, $24,150 head of household): IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32, October 2025, which includes the One Big Beautiful Bill Act changes. Social Security wage base $184,500: SSA 2026 fact sheet. 401(k) limit $24,500, plus $8,000 at 50+ or $11,250 at 60–63: IRS IR-2025-111. From 2026, if your prior-year Social Security wages from the employer were over $150,000, catch-up contributions must be Roth (after-tax); enter those under after-tax deductions.

    The nine states with no tax on wages

    Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington and Wyoming don't tax wages. New Hampshire's old tax on interest and dividends was repealed from January 1, 2025, and Washington taxes only large capital gains. Two of them do take small payroll deductions, which this calculator includes: Washington — WA Cares long-term-care premium, 0.58% of all wages (unless you hold an approved exemption), and the Paid Family & Medical Leave employee share, 71.43% of the 1.13% 2026 premium (0.807%) on wages up to $184,500 (WA ESD). Alaska — unemployment insurance employee contribution, 0.5% of the first $54,200 of wages in 2026 (Alaska DOL). Some cities and counties levy their own taxes; those aren't included.

    What this calculator does not include

    State income tax outside the nine states above, local or city tax (NYC, Philadelphia and others), state disability programs (CA SDI, NJ, NY and others), W-4 entries (extra withholding, dependents, other income), the Child Tax Credit and other credits, itemized deductions, the 2025–2028 deductions for tips, overtime and seniors, AMT, and commuter benefits. It covers the three biggest pieces (federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare) as a working estimate, not a substitute for a pay stub or a tax preparer.

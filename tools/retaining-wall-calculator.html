Retaining Wall Block Calculator

Enter the wall length, the height showing above grade and your block size. You get wall blocks (with the buried course), caps, and the drainage stone and base gravel behind and under the wall.

Wall

Height showing above the finished grade in front of the wall.
Most block makers bury at least one course (or about 1 in per 8 in of wall height, 6 in minimum).

Block

Often the same as the block; some caps are longer.
Cuts at ends, corners and curves. 5% for straight walls, 10% with corners or curves.

Gravel

Block makers usually call for 12 in of clean crushed stone behind the block.
Compacted crushed-stone base; 6 in is typical. Base width = block depth + 12 in.

Estimate

How it works

blocks per course = ceil(wall length ÷ block length); courses = ceil(exposed height ÷ block height) + buried courses; then the waste factor is added. Caps run one course on top: ceil(wall length ÷ cap length).

Drainage stone fills a zone behind the wall for its full built height (exposed plus buried): length × built height × drainage depth. The base trench is length × (block depth + 12 in) × base depth, so the base extends 6 in past the block front and back. Gravel weight uses about 1.4 tons/yd³ for clean crushed stone.

Height limits and engineering

Walls retaining more than 48 in (4 ft) of soil need an engineered design under IRC R404.4, and many jurisdictions require a permit at a lower height, often 30–36 in. Slopes above the wall, driveways, pools or buildings near the top add load and tighten the rules everywhere. Taller walls usually need geogrid reinforcement and inspection. Check with your local building department before you dig.

The base is the wall

The most important part of a segmental wall is a level, well-compacted base of crushed stone. Building on loose native soil is how walls lean and fail. This calculator counts materials; for the base and footing design, follow the block maker's installation guide or a landscape engineer.

Sources: 2021 IRC R404.4; typical segmental-block installation guides (buried course, 12 in drainage zone, 6 in compacted base). Checked November 2026.

How it works

area = length × width + π × radius² + ½ × base × height. rolls = ceil(area × (1 + waste) ÷ roll coverage) and pallets = ceil(rolls ÷ rolls per pallet). Many sod farms sell part pallets, so the result also shows full pallets plus loose rolls.

Measuring an odd-shaped lawn

Split the lawn into simple shapes and add them up; small irregularities are covered by the waste factor. For a really odd shape, a satellite-map measuring tool or graph paper helps.

Roll and pallet sizes

Most residential sod comes as 2 × 5 ft rolls (10 sq ft), 45–50 rolls to a pallet. Big rolls for machine installation are larger. Sizes vary by farm and region, so check before ordering; every number above can be changed.

Install and care

Sod is perishable. Lay it within about a day of delivery, water right away, and keep the soil moist for the first two weeks. Stay off it for about two weeks, then mow lightly.

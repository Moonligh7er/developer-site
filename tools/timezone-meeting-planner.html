Time Zone Meeting Planner

Pick 2–5 cities and a date to see the day lined up hour by hour, with each city's working hours highlighted and the hours that suit everyone listed. Choose a time to get the same moment in every zone, ready to paste. Time zones come from your browser's built-in IANA time-zone data, so daylight-saving changes are applied for the date you pick.

Processing happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

Why the date matters

  
Time zones aren't fixed offsets. Many places shift by an hour for daylight saving time, on different dates: the US changes on the second Sunday of March and first Sunday of November, the EU on the last Sundays of March and October, and much of the Southern Hemisphere the other way round. Others (most of Asia, Arizona) never change. So the gap between two cities can be different in different weeks, and on the changeover day itself one city's day has 23 or 25 hours, which the grid shows as one column fewer or more.

  
The tool works out each city's local time with the browser's Intl.DateTimeFormat API and IANA time-zone names such as America/New_York. There are no bundled tables, so results follow the time-zone data your browser ships with; keeping the browser updated keeps rule changes current. Places with half-hour or 45-minute offsets (India, Nepal, parts of Australia) show their real minutes.

  
Working-hours highlighting

  
An hour is highlighted when the whole hour falls inside the working hours you set, in that city's local time. Columns where every city is highlighted are listed above the grid. When there aren't any (common for teams spread across many zones), the summary shows the hours that suit the most people.
